# Data and figures: sources and terms

This prototype uses no buyer or loan data. Its loan book is synthetic, made from a fixed seed. Every figure below is cited at its source, on the source's own terms.

## ISTAT (Italian National Institute of Statistics)
- Sport practice by region: ISTAT, "La pratica sportiva in Italia, anno 2024", Tavola 2, https://www.istat.it/comunicato-stampa/la-pratica-sportiva-in-italia/
- Cultural participation outside the home by region: ISTAT, Rapporto BES 2025, chapter 2 "Istruzione e formazione", regional table (2024 data), https://www.istat.it/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/02-Istruzione-e-formazione.pdf
- Terms: ISTAT states that, unless otherwise indicated, all content on its site is under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (https://www.istat.it/note-legali/). Source: ISTAT. The regional participation gap scores are this prototype's own rescaling of these rates.

## Department for Culture, Media and Sport (UK)
- Annual value per adult of regular cultural engagement: Frontier Economics for DCMS, "Health and wellbeing benefits of culture and heritage" (2024), Table 3, 2024 prices, https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/678e2ecf432c55fe2988f615/rpt_-_Frontier_Health_and_Wellbeing_Final_Report_09_12_24_accessible_final.pdf
- Terms: published on GOV.UK, where "All content is available under the Open Government Licence v3.0, except where otherwise stated" (https://www.gov.uk/guidance/culture-and-heritage-capital-research-and-outputs). Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0.

## Sport England
- Annual wellbeing value of an active and a fairly active adult: Sport England, "Social value of sport and physical activity 2023-24", summary report, p. 14 (values) and p. 10 ("All figures are in 2024 prices"), https://www.sportengland.org/research-and-data/research/social-value-and-return-investment-sport-and-physical-activity
- Use: two figures cited with their source, and one sentence quoted from the research page above (its caveat on project use); no other Sport England material is reproduced. Sport England's terms of use: https://www.sportengland.org/terms-of-use

## European Commission
- Social discount rate: European Commission, "Economic Appraisal Vademecum 2021-2027" (2022), pp. 13 and 21, https://jaspers.eib.org/files/library/2021/economic-appraisal-vademecum-2021-2027-general-principles-and-sector-applications.pdf (cited).

## European Investment Bank
- Shadow cost of carbon: EIB Group Climate Bank Roadmap 2021-2025 (2020), Annex 5, Table A6, https://www.eib.org/files/publications/thematic/eib_group_climate_bank_roadmap_en.pdf (cited). That roadmap has since been replaced by the EIB Group Climate Bank Roadmap Phase 2, 2026-2030 (2025), which refers for its shadow cost of carbon to the review in the EIB Group 2023 Climate Bank Roadmap Progress Report (2024); paragraph 17 of that report keeps the current values (Phase 2, p. 17, note 20).

## Price base and conversions
Every money value is in 2024 euros.
- GBP to EUR at purchasing power parity: World Bank indicator PA.NUS.PPP (PPP conversion factor, GDP), 2024, Italy 0.599627 and United Kingdom 0.664153 per international dollar, giving 0.902845 EUR per GBP (footnote in the source: Eurostat-OECD annual PPP), https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/PA.NUS.PPP . The World Bank licenses its datasets under CC BY 4.0 unless labelled otherwise (https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/public-licenses).
- 2016 euros to 2024 euros: Eurostat, HICP annual average index, all items, euro area (prc_hicp_aind, 2015=100, as read; the table may now show a later base year, so pick 2015=100 to see these values): 100.23 in 2016 and 126.07 in 2024, https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/prc_hicp_aind/default/table . Eurostat copyright notice: https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/help/copyright-notice
